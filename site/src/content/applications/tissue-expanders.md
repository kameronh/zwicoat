## The problem

Every silicone implant is walled off in scar. Proteins stick to the silicone first, and the body builds a capsule around the device. That means firmness, distortion and discomfort. At the severe end it tightens into capsular contracture, the most common long-term complication of implant surgery. Reported rates run from about 2 to 39 percent, depending on follow-up, radiation, surgical plane and implant surface (Headon 2015; Aesthetic Surgery Journal Open Forum 2025).

Textured implants were once used to lower contracture. In 2019 the FDA requested a recall of BIOCELL textured implants and expanders, and textured use fell.

{{figure:W17}}

Smooth implants are now the default. They carry roughly three times the contracture rate of textured ones (Zhai 2022). For expanders, fibrosis restricts filling, and infection is a leading cause of early removal (Huang 2015; Churchill 2025). Antibiotic irrigation, no-touch technique and dermal matrix wraps act downstream. None of them changes the silicone surface.

## What ZwiGuard does here

Every result below is a laboratory, mouse, rat or sheep result from a published study. No coated implant or expander has been placed in a person.

**Scar capsule, in mice at six weeks.** The chart above shows it. Around coated silicone sheets the capsule was 27 micrometres, against 82 around uncoated sheets (p = 0.010). A non-zwitterionic PEG-type hydrogel gave 43, not significantly different from uncoated. (Horne 2023, Acta Biomaterialia.) Across ten published measurements in mice, rats and sheep, the reduction ranged from about 45 to about 85 percent.

{{figure:W04}}

**Durability and bacteria.** After six months under the skin of mice, the fibroblast-repelling effect was unchanged, and the film was intact at one year (Horne 2023). In rats challenged with S. aureus, implants carried about 940 bacteria per field uncoated and about 155 coated at day 21 (p < 0.0001). These are laboratory and rat results, not clinical results. (Shen 2021.)

## Why it drops in

ZwiGuard is a coating step on a finished silicone shell. It needs no new mold and no new geometry. One UV step grafts and crosslinks the film, about 7 micrometres thick in the published silicone work. It failed inside the gel before the silicone interface let go (Leigh 2019).

Expanders get hit and stretched. In an internal pendulum impact test, a coated expander showed no visible damage to the coating. [See the durability record.](/durability)

Whether the film stretches with an inflated expander is measured in the feasibility round on your parts. So is adhesion on your silicone grade. In ZwiCoat's own tests on silicone coupons, the coating survived seven of eight sterilization cycles; wet e-beam did not.
