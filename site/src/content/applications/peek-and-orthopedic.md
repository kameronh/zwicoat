## The problem

Infection caused about 25 percent of knee revisions and about 15 percent of hip revisions in US national data (Bozic 2010; Bozic 2009). A two-stage revision for infection costs more than twice an aseptic one (Yao 2021). Staphylococci cause 50 to 60 percent of these infections, and they resist antibiotics once they form a biofilm (Tande 2014; Costerton 1999).

PEEK has its own problems. It is hydrophobic and lacks direct bone contact (Walsh 2015). Most bacterial strains tested adhered more to PEEK than to titanium (Sarfraz 2022). In a pooled analysis of 183 PEEK cranioplasties, 15 percent had complications (Punchak 2017). Antibiotic-loaded bone cement did not significantly change one-year revision for infection in registry data on 1.9 million knee replacements (Leta 2024). On interventional devices, FDA has documented lubricious coatings peeling, flaking and shedding (FDA 2019).

## What ZwiGuard does here

No PEEK data are published. The matrix above shows where ZwiCoat has its own tests on PEEK and other substrates. Every published result below was measured on silicone, or on silicone and platinum arrays.

**Bonding, on silicone.** The film bonds so firmly that it fails inside the gel before the silicone interface lets go. Bond strength rises with the grafting agent until that point (Leigh 2019). The feasibility round measures whether the same holds on your PEEK, titanium or cobalt-chrome part.

{{figure:W31}}

**Bacteria.** S. aureus adhesion fell about 97 percent and S. epidermidis adhesion about 93 percent on coated silicone in vitro. In rats challenged with S. aureus, coated implants carried about 155 bacteria per field at day 21, against about 940 uncoated (p < 0.0001). (Shen 2021.)

{{figure:W05}}

**Scar, in mice, rats and sheep.** Across ten published measurements on silicone and implant arrays, the reduction in scar capsule or fibrosis ranged from about 45 to about 85 percent. The film was intact at one year in mice. None of this was measured on bone or PEEK. (Horne 2023; Peel 2024; Barrera 2026; Ben-Shlomo 2026.)

{{figure:W04}}

**Friction.** Friction against tissue, steel and ceramic was more than 90 percent lower on coated silicone (Bennion 2021).
