## The problem

Reported figures show how much a breathing tube asks of the airway. In a review of nine studies, 83 percent of intubated ICU adults had laryngeal injury (Brodsky 2018). In one prospective ICU study, biofilm was found on 95 percent of endotracheal tubes (Gil-Perotin 2012). Each ventilator-associated pneumonia costs about $40,000 (Zimlichman 2013). In children under 5 in intensive care, each extra five days of intubation raised the risk of subglottic stenosis about 50 percent (Manica 2013).

Silver-coated tubes address infection only. In a Cochrane review they lowered ventilator-associated pneumonia (risk ratio 0.64) but not mortality (1.09). Metal-coated tubes in a meta-analysis of five trials gave 0.71 and 1.05 (Tokmaji 2015; Yang 2024). The forest plot above shows the trials. Silver does not address friction, the foreign body response or mucus plugging. Plugging can block a tracheostomy tube.

## What ZwiGuard does here

No ZwiGuard result on an endotracheal or tracheostomy tube is published. The results below are on silicone and on cochlear implant arrays. The feasibility round measures them on your tube.

**Mucus flow, in silicone tubes.** In a 2.6 millimetre silicone tube, mucus started to flow at 31 degrees of tilt against 48 uncoated (p = 0.0017). Dried mucus plugs cleared in a median 22.5 minutes against 30 (p = 0.024). The effect is largest in wider tubes. The mucus was a non-human model, and the authors call the plug result modest and preliminary. (Horne 2025.)

**Insertion force, in human cadaveric cochleae.** Coated cochlear implant arrays needed about 40 percent less peak force, 64.2 against 37.0 millinewtons (p = 0.001). This is the published insertion-force model. It is not a tube result. (Bennion 2021.)

**Friction and bacteria.** Friction on coated silicone was more than 90 percent lower against steel, ceramic and guinea pig dermis (Bennion 2021), and at least 90 percent lower against guinea pig trachea (Peel 2024). In rats challenged with S. aureus, coated silicone carried about 155 bacteria per field at day 21, against about 940 uncoated (p < 0.0001). (Shen 2021.)

## Why it drops in

The published adhesion data are on silicone, where the film failed inside the gel before the silicone interface let go (Leigh 2019). Bonding to PVC, the usual tube material, is measured in the feasibility round on your tubes. The coating is drug-free, so there is no antimicrobial agent to manage.
