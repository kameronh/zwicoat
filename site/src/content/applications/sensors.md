## The problem

The body's response to a sensor limits its accuracy and its wear life. In mice, an implanted continuous glucose monitor took 24 minutes to reach its peak signal, against 2 minutes for blood glucose. Over 80 percent of that delay came from the fibrous capsule around the sensor (McClatchey 2019). The chart above shows it. The capsule shows up as lag, drift and lost sensitivity. One implantable sensor controls the tissue response with a steroid-eluting collar. That is a finite drug reservoir.

## What ZwiGuard does here

No sensor has been coated and measured in a published ZwiGuard study. The results below are on silicone and on implant arrays.

**Scar capsule, in mice.** Around coated silicone sheets at six weeks the capsule was 27 micrometres, against 82 uncoated (p = 0.010). Around human cochlear implant arrays at one year it was about 60 percent thinner, 47 against 18 micrometres, including over platinum contacts (p = 0.026). The film was intact at one year. (Horne 2023.)

{{figure:W27}}

**Cells and fibrosis, in sheep.** At the round window of the sheep cochlea, coated arrays had fewer cell nuclei, fewer macrophages and less fibrosis than uncoated arrays when the three sheep were pooled. One sheep showed no difference in cells or macrophages. (Ben-Shlomo 2026.)

{{figure:W26}}

**Precedent for the class.** A different zwitterionic coating, poly(MPC), cut glucose sensor error without recalibration from 73 to 17 percent in mice over three days and from 48 to 18 percent in non-diabetic primates over five days (Xie 2018). In diabetic primates the gap was smaller, 32 against 22 percent, and it disappeared with recalibration. That is not a ZwiGuard result. ZwiGuard is a thicker, crosslinked, grafted hydrogel rather than a brush.

## Why it drops in

One coating step goes onto the sensor body. The published silicone films were about 7 micrometres thick (Leigh 2019). A sensor needs the film to stay thin and let glucose through, and the film can be tuned. More crosslinker makes the gel stiffer and less swollen, while the protein, cell and friction benefits are strongest at about 5 to 15 percent crosslinker.

Glucose diffusion through the film and sensor response time are not yet measured. Your bench measures response time on the coated parts. The film is drug-free.
