Build note, W37 (review before publishing): the recorded insertion lengths differ by group (coated traces end at 14.9 to 17.8 mm, uncoated at 14.4 to 23.2 mm, depth taken from the sample index as in plot_data.m), and work is integrated over each full trace. Over the depth every trace reaches (0 to 14 mm) the work is 72 versus 116 mN·mm, 38 percent lower, Welch p = 0.14. Peak force is also reached late in the insertion: the peak within 0 to 14 mm is 13.6 versus 22.5 mN, 39 percent lower, Welch p = 0.20. The grant's 68.4 percent matches the record's untrimmed analysis (integral_comparison.png); the record's current plot_data.m with its two trims gives the 66 percent shown. The README calls column 1 encoder counts; in the files it is the logger's Sample Number at 50 samples/s.
