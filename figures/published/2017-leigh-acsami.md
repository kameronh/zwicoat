## Header

- **Citation:** Leigh BL, Cheng E, Xu L, Andresen C, Hansen MR, Guymon CA. Photopolymerizable Zwitterionic Polymer Patterns Control Cell Adhesion and Guide Neural Growth. Biomacromolecules 2017, 18(8), 2389-2401. Received April 21, 2017; published July 3, 2017. University of Iowa.
- **DOI:** 10.1021/acs.biomac.7b00579
- **Open access:** No. No Creative Commons line is printed. The PDF carries the ACS "Downloaded via UNIV OF UTAH ... see sharingguidelines" notice. Reuse of figures needs ACS permission (RightsLink) and author consent. The Supporting Information (Figures S1, S2) is free on the ACS site but is not in this PDF.
- **Funding:** NIH R01 DC012578, P30 DC010362, T35HL007485-35. Authors declare no competing financial interest.
- **One-sentence finding:** A one-step UV process grafts thin carboxybetaine (CBMA) or sulfobetaine (SBMA) coatings onto glass that cut fibrinogen adsorption to under 10% of bare glass, nearly eliminate fibroblast, astrocyte and Schwann cell attachment (CBMA best), and make nerve fibers grow in straight lines along 100 micrometre stripes.

Substrate: glass (silanized with methacrylate). Monomer 0.1 to 20 wt % in PBS, 0.05 wt % HEPK photoinitiator, 10 min UV at 16 mW/cm2 (365 nm). Cells were rat primary cells (fibroblasts from skin, astrocytes from cortex, Schwann cells from sciatic nerve, spiral ganglion neurons from cochlea), fixed after 48 h. All data are in vitro. Nothing here is an in vivo or implant result.

### Figure 4 (PDF p. 5, journal p. 2393). Fibrinogen adsorption and water contact angle versus monomer concentration
- **Shows:** (A) SBMA and (B) CBMA. Bars: relative protein adsorption (left axis, bare silanized glass = 1.0). Dots: water contact angle (right axis, degrees).
- **Conditions compared:** SBMA vs CBMA at about 0, 0.1, 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2.5, 10 wt % monomer (x axis is broken between 2.5 and 10).
- **Numbers, exact from text:** silanized glass contact angle "over 57 degrees". SBMA contact angle plateaus at about 15 degrees; CBMA plateaus at about 11 degrees (above 1 wt %). SBMA protein adsorption drops sharply up to 0.5 wt % and is below 10% of the control at higher concentrations. CBMA reaches about 6% of control; the CBMA vs SBMA difference was not statistically significant. Contact angle plateau reached about 1 wt %; protein adsorption minimum reached about 0.5 wt %.
- **Numbers, read from figure, approximate:**

| Monomer wt % | SBMA protein (rel.) | SBMA angle (deg) | CBMA protein (rel.) | CBMA angle (deg) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 1.00 | 57 | 1.00 | 57 |
| about 0.1 | 0.85 | 40 | 0.90 | 51 |
| about 0.25 | 0.55 | 33 | 0.72 | 44 |
| 0.5 | 0.12 | 19 | 0.09 | 19 |
| 1 | 0.14 | 14 | 0.08 | 16 |
| 2.5 | 0.09 | 17 | 0.07 | 12 |
| 10 | 0.08 | 15 | 0.07 | 12 |

  The 0 wt % angle (57) is the text's silanized-glass value; the plot's first dot sits near 54. Intermediate angle values are rough.
- **n per group:** protein: samples made in triplicate, 5 images per condition (Methods). Contact angle: 5 areas per substrate, in triplicate, 15 measurements averaged. Error bars shown; SEM not explicitly stated for this figure.
- **Statistic:** none given beyond the remark that CBMA vs SBMA protein adsorption was not significantly different.
- **Units:** protein relative to bare glass (unitless, fluorescence intensity); contact angle in degrees.

### Figure 6 (PDF p. 7, journal p. 2395). Fibroblast adhesion
- **Shows:** (E) fibroblast density on uniformly coated glass. (F) density on 100 micrometre striped substrates, uncoated bands vs coated bands. 48 h culture.
- **Conditions compared:** (E) glass, SBMA, CBMA. (F) SBMA stripes and CBMA stripes, uncoated vs coated.
- **Numbers, exact from text:** SBMA uniform: "less than half" the density of glass. CBMA uniform: "less than one percent" of glass. SBMA stripes: "nearly 40% reduction" coated vs uncoated. CBMA stripes: "zero fibroblasts" on coated stripes. Coating still repelled cells after a second seeding at 48 h plus 24 h (Figure S1, in the Supporting Information, not in this PDF).
- **Numbers, read from figure, approximate (cells per square micrometre x 10^-4):**
  - (E) glass 9.0; SBMA 4.2 (error bar to about 5.4); CBMA about 0.05 (barely visible).
  - (F) SBMA stripes: uncoated 11.8, coated 4.4. CBMA stripes: uncoated 9.7, coated 0.
- **n per group:** at least 10 fields per sample, at least 3 samples per condition, experiments repeated at least 3 times. Exact n not stated in the figure.
- **Statistic:** (E) all groups differ, p < 0.001, one-way ANOVA with Tukey. (F) coated vs uncoated, p < 0.001, t test, for both SBMA and CBMA; SBMA vs CBMA stripes p < 0.001, one-way ANOVA. Error bars SEM.
- **Units:** cells per square micrometre x 10^-4, as printed.

### Figure 7 (PDF p. 8, journal p. 2396). Astrocyte adhesion
- **Shows:** (E) astrocyte density on uniform coatings. (F) on stripes.
- **Numbers, exact from text:** SBMA uniform: 13-fold reduction vs glass. CBMA uniform: decrease "of over 90-fold". SBMA stripes: 75% reduction on coated vs uncoated. CBMA coated stripes: "about 20-fold" lower than uncoated glass. Astrocyte culture purity above 98% (GFAP).
- **Numbers, read from figure, approximate (x 10^-4 cells/um2):** (E) glass 6.1; SBMA 0.45; CBMA about 0.05. (F) SBMA stripes: uncoated 5.3, coated 1.2. CBMA stripes: uncoated 1.9, coated about 0.1.
- **Note:** the "about 20-fold" statement and the "over 90-fold" statement for CBMA do not agree with each other or exactly with the bars. Use plotted values with a caveat, or quote the text as printed.
- **n:** as for Figure 6. **Statistic:** (E) glass vs SBMA and CBMA p < 0.001, one-way ANOVA. (F) coated vs uncoated p < 0.001 t test; SBMA vs CBMA stripes p < 0.001. SEM.

### Figure 8 (PDF p. 8, journal p. 2396). Schwann cell adhesion
- **Shows:** (E) uniform coatings. (F) stripes.
- **Numbers, exact from text:** SBMA uniform: "more than a 40 fold decrease". CBMA uniform: "over 80x decrease". SBMA stripes: 13-fold lower on coated than uncoated. CBMA stripes: "over 150-fold" lower on coated than uncoated.
- **Numbers, read from figure, approximate (x 10^-4 cells/um2):** (E) glass 14.0 (error bar to about 16.8); SBMA 0.3; CBMA about 0.15. (F) SBMA stripes: uncoated 13.7, coated 1.0. CBMA stripes: uncoated 11.0, coated about 0.07.
- **n:** as for Figure 6. **Statistic:** (E) coated vs glass p < 0.001, one-way ANOVA. (F) coated vs uncoated p < 0.001, t test. SEM.

### Figure 9 (PDF p. 9, journal p. 2397). Schwann cell alignment to stripes
- **Shows:** average angle between the long axis of each Schwann cell and the stripe direction. 0 degrees is parallel; random is expected to give 45 degrees.
- **Conditions:** glass (no pattern), SBMA stripes, CBMA stripes, 100 micrometre period.
- **Numbers, exact from text:** glass about 42 degrees; SBMA "less than 6 degrees"; CBMA "just under 2 degrees".
- **Numbers, read from figure:** glass 41.5, SBMA 5.4, CBMA 1.8 degrees.
- **n per group (printed on figure):** glass 207 cells, SBMA 236, CBMA 207.
- **Statistic:** all groups differ, p < 0.001, one-way ANOVA (Methods also describe Kruskal-Wallis with Dunn's test). SEM.
- **Units:** degrees.

### Figure 10 (PDF p. 10, journal p. 2398). Spiral ganglion neurite alignment
- **Shows:** alignment ratio = total neurite path length / length aligned with the pattern. 1.0 is perfectly straight along the stripe; larger values wander.
- **Numbers, exact from text:** glass "over 2.2"; SBMA stripes "approximately 1.5"; CBMA stripes 1.09.
- **Numbers, read from figure:** glass 2.22, SBMA 1.52, CBMA 1.09.
- **n per group (printed on figure):** glass 79 neurites, SBMA 90, CBMA 40.
- **Statistic:** all groups differ, p < 0.001, one-way ANOVA (Kruskal-Wallis with Dunn's test). SEM.
- **Units:** ratio (unitless).

## Candidate website charts

1. **"How much protein sticks to the coating"**
   - Chart type: grouped line or bar chart, protein adsorption vs monomer concentration, two series (SBMA, CBMA), plus a dashed 100% bare-glass reference.
   - Data series: Figure 4 protein values listed above (0 to 10 wt %).
   - Data status: **read from figure, approximate** for each point. Text gives exact statements only for the end points (below 10% for SBMA, about 6% for CBMA).
   - Device relevance: protein adsorption is the first step of the foreign body response.
2. **"Fibroblast attachment on bare glass vs coated glass"**
   - Chart type: three-bar chart (glass, SBMA, CBMA), shown relative to glass (100%), log scale optional because CBMA is near zero.
   - Data series: Figure 6E. Glass about 9.0, SBMA about 4.2, CBMA about 0.05 (x 10^-4 cells/um2).
   - Data status: bar heights **read from figure**; the ratios "less than half" and "less than 1%" are **exact from text**. A relative-to-glass chart can be built from the text alone.
   - Device relevance: fibroblasts make the fibrotic capsule around implants.
3. **"Cells avoid the coated stripes"**
   - Chart type: grouped bars, uncoated band vs coated band, for fibroblasts, astrocytes and Schwann cells on CBMA stripes (optionally SBMA too).
   - Data series: Figures 6F, 7F, 8F values above.
   - Data status: **read from figure**; fold changes (about 40% SBMA fibroblast, 75% SBMA astrocyte, 13-fold and over 150-fold Schwann) **exact from text**.
4. **"Nerve fibers follow the coated pattern"**
   - Chart type: three-bar chart of alignment ratio (glass, SBMA, CBMA), reference line at 1.0 = perfect.
   - Data series: 2.22, 1.52, 1.09 with n = 79, 90, 40.
   - Data status: **exact from text** for "over 2.2", "approximately 1.5", 1.09; the decimals for glass and SBMA are read from the figure. Relevance: neural electrode and cochlear implant interface.
5. **"Thin coatings reach full wetting at low monomer concentration"** (supporting)
   - Chart type: two-panel line chart, contact angle vs monomer wt %.
   - Data series: Figure 4 contact angle values. Data status: **read from figure**; plateau values (about 15 and 11 degrees) **exact from text**.

Caution for website use: all of these are in vitro cell culture on glass. They support "reduces protein and cell attachment" claims only, not any implant or clinical outcome.
