# Leigh et al. 2019: antifouling photograftable zwitterionic coatings on PDMS

## Header

- **Citation:** Leigh BL, Cheng E, Xu L, Derk A, Hansen MR, Guymon CA. Antifouling Photograftable Zwitterionic Coatings on PDMS Substrates. Langmuir 2019, 35(4), 1100-1110. Received March 13, 2018; revised June 27, 2018; published July 9, 2018 (Special Issue: Zwitterionic Interfaces). University of Iowa.
- **DOI:** 10.1021/acs.langmuir.8b00838
- **Open access:** No. No Creative Commons line is printed. The PDF carries the ACS "Downloaded via UNIV OF IOWA ... see sharingguidelines" notice. Figure reuse needs ACS permission and author consent.
- **Funding and conflicts:** NIH R01-DC012578, T32DC000040, T35HL007485-35 and P30-DC010362; A. Derk supported by an NSF REU grant (NSF-1757548). Authors declare no competing financial interest.
- **One-sentence finding:** A one-step UV process grafts a cross-linked zwitterionic hydrogel (about 7 micrometres thick) onto silicone (PDMS) so firmly that it fails inside the gel rather than at the silicone interface, cuts fibrinogen adsorption by more than 90%, and cuts fibroblast attachment 20-fold or more, still holding after 14 days in culture medium.

Substrate: PDMS (Sylgard 184, 10:1, cured 80 C for 2 h). Coating bath: 50 wt % SBMA or CBMA, 2.5 wt % PEGDA (MW 575) cross-linker, 1 wt % HEPK photoinitiator, 46.5 wt % water. Benzophenone (BP) adsorbed on PDMS from acetone (0.01 to 50 mg/mL feed) is the grafting agent. UV 30 mW/cm2 at 365 nm for 10 min. All data are in vitro.

## Figures and tables

There are no numbered tables. Figures 1, 2 and 5 are schematics (process, chemistry, shear test). Figure 4 is SEM images.

### Figure 3 (PDF p. 4, journal p. 1103). Benzophenone on the PDMS surface vs benzophenone in the bath
- **Shows:** BP surface concentration (UV/vis after extraction, 253 nm) vs BP feed concentration. Log y axis.
- **Conditions:** feed 0.25 to 50 mg/mL (axis printed as g/L).
- **Numbers, exact from text:** adsorbed BP ranged "from just under 1 microgram/cm2 to over 500 micrograms/cm2". Crystals form on the surface above 50 mg/mL, so that was the limit. Calibration R2 = 0.9989.
- **Numbers, read from figure, approximate (ug/cm2):** 0.25 mg/mL: 0.9; 0.5: 2.5; 0.75: 6.5; 1.0: 9; 2.5: 21; 5: 55; 10: 110; 50: about 450 (the top point reads about 400 to 500, so "over 500" in the text may be rounding up or a slightly different reading).
- **n:** not stated. Error bars not visible. **Statistic:** none.
- **Units:** micrograms per square centimetre.

### Figure 4 (PDF p. 5, journal p. 1104). SEM cross-sections of the coating on PDMS
- **Shows:** (A, B) pSBMA and pCBMA at 2.5 mg/mL BP feed: coating peeling from PDMS. (C, D) at 50 mg/mL: no delamination.
- **Numbers (text, p. 5):** film thickness pSBMA 7.3 (plus or minus 2.8) micrometres; pCBMA 7.5 (plus or minus 1.7) micrometres; independent of BP concentration. Whether the plus or minus is SD is not stated. n for thickness not stated.
- **Statistic:** none.

### Figure 6 (PDF p. 6, journal p. 1105). Shear adhesion strength vs benzophenone feed concentration
- **Shows:** maximum force per unit area at break in a shear test (DMA force ramp 0.5 N/min, room temperature) for (A) pSBMA and (B) pCBMA films bonded to PDMS, as prepared (open symbols) and after 48 h swelling in water (filled symbols). Shaded region = cohesive failure (the gel itself tears, interface intact).
- **Conditions:** BP feed 0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1, 2.5, 5, 10, 50 mg/mL; SBMA vs CBMA; as prepared vs swollen.
- **Numbers, exact from text:** SBMA: adhesive failure below 2.5 mg/mL, cohesive failure at or above 2.5 mg/mL. CBMA: cohesive failure at 5 mg/mL and above. Many swollen samples below 2.5 mg/mL "fractured adhesively before sample loading and were recorded as 0 N/m2". No significant change in force above the cohesive threshold. pCBMA values at high BP exceed pSBMA; authors attribute this to pCBMA forming a higher modulus hydrogel (cited, not measured here). The conclusion says the critical concentration lies "between 1 and 2.5 g/L".
- **Numbers, read from figure, approximate (N/m2 x 10^4):**

| BP feed (mg/mL) | SBMA as prepared | SBMA swollen | CBMA as prepared | CBMA swollen |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0.9 | 0 | 2.1 | 0 |
| 0.25 | 3.9 | 1.0 | 2.1 | 0.9 |
| 0.5 | 4.3 | 1.5 | 4.6 | 0.4 |
| 0.75 | 5.4 | 1.5 | 5.8 | 2.5 |
| 1.0 | 5.0 | 0.5 | 4.4 | 1.1 |
| 2.5 | 5.2 | 3.4 | 5.6 | 3.9 |
| 5 | 6.1 | 3.9 | 7.1 | 4.4 |
| 10 | 7.4 | 3.7 | 8.5 | 5.6 |
| 50 | 7.1 | 3.5 | 10.0 | 6.2 |

  For orientation, 10^4 N/m2 = 10 kPa. Error bars are large (for example CBMA as prepared at 50 mg/mL spans about 7.5 to 12.5).
- **n per group:** not stated in the text or caption. **Statistic:** none given. Error bar type not stated.
- **Units:** N/m2 x 10^4 as printed.
- **Inconsistencies to be aware of:** the text says cohesive failure for SBMA starts "at or above 2.5 mg/mL" while also saying films "at or less than 2.5 mg/mL" peeled in SEM (Figure 4). The figure shading for SBMA begins at 2.5 mg/mL. Treat 2.5 mg/mL as the transition for SBMA and 5 mg/mL for CBMA.

### Figure 7 (PDF p. 7, journal p. 1106). Fibrinogen adsorption
- **Shows:** (A to C) fluorescence images of adsorbed fibrinogen on uncoated, pSBMA and pCBMA PDMS. (D) quantified relative fluorescence, uncoated = 1.0.
- **Conditions:** uncoated PDMS, pSBMA-coated, pCBMA-coated (50 mg/mL BP feed). 1 mg/mL human fibrinogen, 1 h.
- **Numbers, exact from text:** "greater than 90% reduction in fibrinogen adsorption" for both coatings (conclusion, p. 8). pSBMA vs pCBMA not different (one-way ANOVA p = 0.9585).
- **Numbers, read from figure, approximate:** uncoated 1.0 (error bar to about 1.17); pSBMA about 0.02; pCBMA about 0.06.
- **n:** all conditions in triplicate, five images per condition (Methods). **Statistic:** coated vs uncoated p < 0.001, one-way ANOVA. Error bars are **standard deviation** here (caption), unlike the cell figures.

### Figure 8 (PDF p. 7, journal p. 1106). Fibroblast adhesion without serum pre-soak
- **Shows:** fibroblast density after 48 h on uncoated, pSBMA, pCBMA PDMS, no FBS.
- **Numbers, exact from text:** pSBMA "less than 5%" of the cells on PDMS control; pCBMA "just above 3%". Coated surfaces show rounded cells with few extensions.
- **Numbers, read from figure, approximate (cells/um2 x 10^-4):** uncoated 7.8 (error bar to 8.5); pSBMA 0.4; pCBMA 0.25.
- **n:** at least 10 images per culture, at least 3 substrates per experiment, repeated in triplicate (Methods). 0.5 mL at 1 x 10^7 cells/mL seeded on 10 mm discs.
- **Statistic:** coated vs uncoated p < 0.001, one-way ANOVA with Tukey. SEM.

### Figure 9 (PDF p. 8, journal p. 1107). Fibroblast adhesion after serum (FBS) pre-soak
- **Shows:** as Figure 8 but samples soaked in FBS before seeding (harder test).
- **Numbers, exact from text:** serum pre-soak "did not dramatically alter" cell density. pSBMA vs pCBMA not significantly different (p = 0.8489). pSBMA had slightly more cells than pCBMA.
- **Numbers, read from figure, approximate (x 10^-4 cells/um2):** uncoated 7.4 (error bar to 8.3); pSBMA 0.6; pCBMA 0.15.
- **n and statistic:** as Figure 8; coated vs uncoated p < 0.001.

### Figure 10 (PDF p. 8, journal p. 1107). Fibroblast adhesion after 14 days in culture medium
- **Shows:** fibroblast density at day 0 and after coated samples sat 14 days in culture medium.
- **Numbers, exact from text (p. 8):** uncoated PDMS "approximately 11 x 10^-4 to 13 x 10^-4 cells/um2"; pSBMA "about 0.6 x 10^-4 to 1.3 x 10^-4"; pCBMA "from no observable cells to 0.6 x 10^-4", "almost 20 times less than" the PDMS control. Increase over 14 days not statistically significant for any substrate. Films remained "securely adhered" for the whole experiment.
- **Numbers, read from figure, approximate (x 10^-4 cells/um2):** day 0: uncoated 26, pSBMA 1.2, pCBMA 0. Day 14: uncoated 30, pSBMA 2.8, pCBMA 1.2.
- **CONFLICT:** the plotted bar heights are about 2.3 times the values quoted in the text (text 11 and 13 vs figure about 26 and 30; text 0.6 and 1.3 vs figure about 1.2 and 2.8). Ratios between groups are similar either way (coating roughly 20 to 25 times lower). Do not publish absolute cell densities from this figure without asking the authors which is correct. Use ratios.
- **n:** as Figure 8. **Statistic:** coated vs uncoated significant at both times, p < 0.001 (asterisk; exact p printed only as "*"). SEM.

## Numbers quoted in the text

Page numbers are PDF pages (journal page in brackets).

- p. 1 [1100], abstract: shear adhesion increases with benzophenone concentration; failure mode changes from adhesive delamination to cohesive failure at higher BP; "significantly lower" protein adsorption; fibroblast adhesion "dramatically reduced" (no numbers in abstract).
- p. 1 to 2 [1100 to 1101]: PDMS is hydrophobic and prone to protein adsorption (background).
- p. 3 [1102], methods: PDMS 10:1 base to curing agent, 80 C for 2 h; BP feed 0.01 to 50 mg/mL; coating bath 50 wt % monomer, 2.5 wt % PEGDA, 1 wt % HEPK, 46.5 wt % water; 2.5 microlitre of solution forms an 8 mm x 8 mm contact area; UV 30 mW/cm2, 10 min; swollen samples 48 h in water; force ramp 0.5 N/min; fibrinogen 1 mg/mL for 1 h; fibroblast seeding 1 x 10^7 cells/mL, 0.5 mL, 48 h.
- p. 3 [1102], results: functional coating "thin film (<25 micrometres)" is the authors' framing.
- p. 4 [1103]: adsorbed BP from just under 1 to over 500 micrograms/cm2; crystals above 50 mg/mL; calibration R2 = 0.9989 (methods, p. 3).
- p. 5 [1104]: peeling at or below 2.5 mg/mL BP; film thickness pSBMA 7.3 (plus or minus 2.8) micrometres, pCBMA 7.5 (plus or minus 1.7) micrometres.
- p. 6 [1105]: SBMA cohesive failure at or above 2.5 mg/mL; CBMA cohesive failure at 5 mg/mL and above; zero recorded for early-failing swollen samples; gel made with 50% water from the bath then swollen further. 50 mg/mL BP used for all antifouling tests.
- p. 7 [1106]: fibrinogen p = 0.9585 (SBMA vs CBMA).
- p. 8 [1107 to 1108]: fibroblast density pSBMA under 5% and pCBMA just above 3% of control (no FBS); p = 0.8489 (with FBS); 11 to 13, 0.6 to 1.3 and 0 to 0.6 (x 10^-4 cells/um2) over 14 days; pCBMA "almost 20 times" lower than control.
- p. 8 [1108], conclusions: greater than 90% reduction in fibrinogen; fibroblast adhesion reduced "20-fold or greater"; critical BP concentration "between 1 and 2.5 g/L"; coatings resisted fibroblasts "up to 14 days in vitro".

## Candidate website charts

1. **"The coating stays on: bond strength rises with grafting agent until the gel tears first"**
   - Chart type: two-panel dot-and-line chart (pSBMA, pCBMA), force per area vs benzophenone concentration, as-prepared and swollen series, shaded band where failure moves from the interface to inside the gel.
   - Data series: Figure 6 values above (8 or 9 concentrations x 2 hydration states x 2 chemistries).
   - Data status: **read from figure, approximate**. Failure thresholds (2.5 mg/mL SBMA, 5 mg/mL CBMA) are **exact from text**. Large error bars should be drawn. Relevance: durability and adhesion of a coating on silicone devices, which is the first concern for an R&D lead.
2. **"Protein sticking to silicone, before and after coating"**
   - Chart type: three-bar chart (uncoated PDMS = 100%, pSBMA, pCBMA).
   - Data series: Figure 7D; uncoated 1.0, pSBMA about 0.02, pCBMA about 0.06.
   - Data status: bar values **read from figure**; "greater than 90% reduction" is **exact from text**, so a safe version of the chart can be built from the text alone as ">90% lower".
3. **"Fibroblast attachment on silicone, with and without a serum pre-soak"**
   - Chart type: grouped bars (uncoated, pSBMA, pCBMA; two groups: no FBS, with FBS), or relative to uncoated.
   - Data series: Figure 8D and 9D values above.
   - Data status: **read from figure**; the percentages "under 5%" and "just above 3%" (no FBS) are **exact from text**.
4. **"Antifouling holds after 14 days soaking"**
   - Chart type: grouped bars, day 0 vs day 14, three surfaces. Or show the ratio to uncoated only.
   - Data series: Figure 10D, or the text values 11 to 13, 0.6 to 1.3, 0 to 0.6 (x 10^-4 cells/um2).
   - Data status: text values are **exact**, but they conflict with the plotted bar heights (about 2.3 times higher). Ask the authors before charting absolute values; a ratio chart is safe.
5. **"How much grafting agent sits on the surface"** (supporting, lower priority)
   - Chart type: scatter, log y.
   - Data series: Figure 3 values. Data status: **read from figure** except the range endpoints (text).

Caution for website use: coatings are tested on a lab PDMS slab, in vitro, with a hydrogel about 7 micrometres thick. The paper states no in vivo result, no friction, no insertion force, and no bacterial result.
