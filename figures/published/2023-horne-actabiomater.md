# Horne et al. 2023, Acta Biomaterialia (foreign body response, six weeks to one year in mice)

## Header

- Citation: Horne R, Ben-Shlomo N, Jensen M, Ellerman M, Escudero C, Hua R, Bennion D, Guymon CA, Hansen MR. Reducing the foreign body response on human cochlear implants and their materials in vivo with photografted zwitterionic hydrogel coatings. Acta Biomaterialia 166:212-223, 2023.
- DOI: 10.1016/j.actbio.2023.05.011
- Open access: NO evidence of it. The page shows "(c) 2023 Acta Materialia Inc. Published by Elsevier Ltd. All rights reserved." No CC licence line is printed on any page. Treat as permission needed for figures. Supplementary material (embedding protocol) is online at the DOI.
- Coating chemistry: pCBMA, poly(carboxybetaine methacrylate), NOT the SBMA sulfobetaine used in Bennion 2021 and Peel 2024. Cross-linker PEGDMA (400 g/mol). Photografted onto PDMS sheets, PDMS tubes (1.2 mm outer diameter), and Advanced Bionics mid-scala and Cochlear lateral wall human implant arrays. Only the CI arrays are coated at 10 wt% cross-linker; sheets and tubes vary.
- Animals: CBA/J mice, 8 to 10 weeks, subcutaneous implants (BL/6 mice for the partially coated implant, Fig. 4). Equal males and females. Subcutaneous, not in a cochlea.
- Disclosures printed: authors list a patent and pending applications on "durable photopolymerizable cross-linked anti-fouling coatings".
- Plain-language finding: Zwitterionic coated silicone and cochlear implant electrode arrays grew a scar capsule that was about 40 to 70 percent thinner than uncoated ones in mice, from six weeks out to one year, and the coating stayed intact.
- Page numbering: PDF page n is printed page 211 + n. All pages below are printed page numbers.

## Figures and tables

There are no numbered tables. Figures 3, 5, 6, 7 carry plotted numbers. Figures 1, 2, 4 are mainly schematic or images.

### Figure 1 (p. 214): coating process schematic

- Shows: drawn scheme of photografting (benzophenone pre-treatment, solvent removal, pre-monomer solution, UV cure) and chemical structures of CBMA, PEGDMA, Irgacure 2959 and benzophenone. No data.
- Recipe (p. 214): 35 wt% monomers, 0.8 wt% surfactant, 0.05 wt% Irgacure 2959, balance water. 10 minutes UV at 30 mW/cm2 under nitrogen. Cross-linker 0 to 100 percent of the monomer fraction. CI arrays coated with 10 wt% cross-linker.

### Figure 2 (p. 215): thin film on PDMS and CI arrays

- Shows: A, photographs of uncoated and coated PDMS strips, with and without fluorescein dye (coated absorbs the green dye, uncoated does not). B and C, SEM cross-sections at 150x of uncoated PDMS and coated PDMS after 6 months in mouse subcutaneous tissue (coating visible and uniform). D and E, photographs of straight CI arrays, uncoated and coated, dyed.
- Numbers: scale bars only (10 mm, 300 micrometres in B and C, 4 mm in D and E). Coating thickness is not stated in numbers.
- Statistic: none.

### Figure 3 (p. 216): fibroblast adhesion in vitro, new and after 6 months implanted

- Shows: A to E, fibroblasts stained green (vimentin) and nuclei blue on uncoated PDMS (A) and coated PDMS at 1.6, 5, 13, 31 percent cross-linker (B to E). F, fibroblast density, normalised to uncoated, for newly made coatings (black) and coatings explanted after 6 months in mice (gray).
- Conditions: uncoated, and CBMA-coated at 1.6, 5, 13, 31 percent cross-linker; each measured "new" and "6-month implant".
- Numbers: Uncoated is set to 1.0. Read from figure F, approximate: new 1.6 percent about 0.18, 5 percent about 0.12, 13 percent about 0.03, 31 percent about 0.14. Six-month implant: 1.6 percent about 0.20, 5 percent about 0.14, 13 percent about 0.11, 31 percent about 0.16. Error bars (SEM) on uncoated about 0.33 (new) and 0.48 (6-month), read from figure.
- Text exact: coated density "an order of magnitude less" than uncoated (p. 217); all coated groups differ from uncoated at p of 0.02 or less by Tukey; new versus 6-month implant not different, p = 0.73.
- n: 3 per condition. Method: mean of nine 20x fields per sample; 48 h culture of cochlear spiral ligament fibroblasts from p2 to p5 mice.
- Statistic: two-way ANOVA with Tukey post hoc. The in-figure label says "* p<0.05" and the caption says p < 0.02; use the caption/text value only after checking.
- Units: dimensionless (fibroblast count density normalised to uncoated).

### Figure 4 (p. 217): coated and uncoated regions on the same implant, 6 weeks

- Shows: H&E sections of a PDMS slab, half uncoated and half 5 wt% cross-linked CBMA coated, 6 weeks in mouse subcutaneous tissue. A is a wide view, B is a close view. Yellow dotted lines outline the capsule; arrows mark thickness.
- Numbers: none plotted. Scale bars 200 and 100 micrometres. Text says the intense cellular reaction beside the coating lasts only a few hundred micrometres and then fades; the Discussion adds that regions beyond about 600 micrometres from uncoated sites look protected (p. 221).
- Statistic: none.
- This is the single best qualitative image: one implant, two sides.

### Figure 5 (p. 218): capsule thickness on PDMS sheets, 6 weeks (flat geometry)

- Shows: A, uncoated PDMS. B, 100 percent PEGDMA (pPEGDMA) coating. C, 10 wt% cross-linked pCBMA coating. D, mean capsule thickness with individual points.
- Conditions: uncoated PDMS, pPEGDMA (non-zwitterionic control), pCBMA (10 percent cross-linker). A 10 percent pHEMA film group caused skin ulceration and was stopped within a week (no histology).
- Numbers (exact, text p. 218): uncoated 82 micrometres, pPEGDMA 43 micrometres, pCBMA 27 micrometres (mean).
- Figure D bar tops, read from figure, approximate: uncoated about 84, pPEGDMA about 47, pCBMA about 26 micrometres (these differ slightly from the text; use the text). Error bars are SEM: uncoated about 25 to 28, pPEGDMA about 17, pCBMA about 11 (read from figure). Individual points (read from figure, approximate): uncoated about 117, 104, 85, 64, 55; pPEGDMA about 71, 40, 40, 32; pCBMA about 39, 22, 20.
- n: 3 to 5 per group (the figure shows 5 uncoated, 4 pPEGDMA, 3 pCBMA points).
- Statistic: one-way ANOVA significant; Tukey: pCBMA versus uncoated p = 0.010; pPEGDMA versus uncoated p = 0.061 (not significant).
- Derived by us from the text means, not stated: pCBMA 67 percent thinner than uncoated; pPEGDMA 48 percent thinner.
- Units: micrometres.

### Figure 6 (p. 220): capsule thickness versus cross-linker percent, cylindrical PDMS, 6 weeks

- Shows: A to F, H&E images of capsules on uncoated PDMS (A), 0 percent cross-linker coating (B), and 5, 10, 25, 50 percent cross-linker (C to F). G, mean capsule thickness versus cross-linker percent.
- Conditions: uncoated PDMS cylinder (1.2 mm outer diameter tube) versus CBMA coating at 0, 5, 10, 25, 50 wt% cross-linker.
- Numbers: read from figure G, approximate, mean in micrometres: uncoated about 25.5; 0 percent about 28; 5 percent about 14.7; 10 percent about 14; 25 percent about 17.4; 50 percent about 18.3. SEM bars, read from figure: about 5 (uncoated), about 5 (0%), about 3 (5%), about 4 (10%), about 4.7 (25%), about 8.5 (50%). The paper prints no table of these means.
- Text and caption exact: one-way ANOVA p = 0.0044. Dunnett versus uncoated: 0 percent p = 0.3322 (not significant); 5 percent p = 0.0039; 10 percent p = 0.0031; 25 percent p = 0.0031; 50 percent p = 0.0265. The 0 percent group (no cross-linker) is not different from uncoated.
- Text: capsule on cross-linked coatings reduced "by a factor of 2" (p. 219). Our derived reading is about 30 to 45 percent thinner (5 percent about 42 percent, 10 percent about 45 percent, 25 percent about 32 percent, 50 percent about 28 percent), so a factor of 2 is the upper end.
- n: 3 per group. Error bars SEM.
- Units: micrometres.
- Note: uncoated capsule on cylinders (about 25 micrometres) is much thinner than on flat sheets (82 micrometres, Fig. 5). Different geometry. Do not plot Figures 5 and 6 on the same axis.

### Figure 7 (p. 221): one-year capsule on human cochlear implant arrays

- Shows: A and B, photographs of uncoated and coated mid-scala arrays after one year in subcutaneous mouse tissue. C and D, resin-embedded arrays cut through the platinum electrode face. E to H, 1 micrometre sections stained Toluidine blue: uncoated platinum (E), coated platinum (F), uncoated PDMS housing (G), coated PDMS housing (H). I, capsule thickness for PDMS and Pt regions, uncoated versus coated.
- Numbers (exact, text p. 220): mean capsule thickness 47 micrometres for uncoated, 18 micrometres for coated, "60% less". Capsule on the uncoated platinum looks pyramidal and thick; coated platinum has a minimal fibrosis band.
- Figure I, read from figure, approximate (mean in micrometres): uncoated PDMS about 37, uncoated Pt about 57, coated PDMS about 19, coated Pt about 17. Individual uncoated points up to about 100 micrometres (one Pt value). Error bars SEM. The text says PDMS versus platinum surfaces did not differ statistically, though the platinum appears to provoke a stronger response (p. 220).
- n: 3 to 4 per bar.
- Statistic: two-way ANOVA, coated versus uncoated p = 0.026.
- Units: micrometres.
- Text: "Very little variability was observed in capsule thickness for coated samples. On the other hand, variability was high in uncoated samples."

Cautions for the site:

- The paper's claims about hearing, impedance and battery life are discussion, not results. The site rules say performance claims only.
- The "50 to 70 percent" headline is an upper-end summary. Our reading of Figure 6 gives about 30 to 45 percent for cylinders; the 67 percent (27 versus 82 micrometres) is the flat-sheet result and the 60 percent is the one-year CI result.
- The paper reports no macrophage or giant cell counts, only fibroblast density (in vitro) and capsule thickness (in vivo). Cellular density is described qualitatively in the histology text.
