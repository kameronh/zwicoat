# Jensen et al. 2021: antifouling and mechanical properties depend on cross-link density

## Header

- **Citation:** Jensen MJ, Peel A, Horne R, Chamberlain J, Xu L, Hansen MR, Guymon CA. Antifouling and Mechanical Properties of Photografted Zwitterionic Hydrogel Thin-Film Coatings Depend on the Cross-Link Density. ACS Biomaterials Science and Engineering 2021, 7(9), 4494-4502. Received June 27, 2021; accepted July 22, 2021; published August 4, 2021. University of Iowa.
- **DOI:** 10.1021/acsbiomaterials.1c00852 (https://doi.org/10.1021/acsbiomaterials.1c00852)
- **Open access:** Yes. Page 1 shows the ACS open-access padlock and Creative Commons icons, and the copyright line reads "2021 The Authors. Published by American Chemical Society". The text of the license variant is not printed in the PDF text layer. The icons look like CC BY. **Confirm the exact license on the ACS article page before reuse.** If it is CC BY, figures can be reused with attribution without asking permission. This is the best paper of the four for reuse.
- **Funding and conflicts:** NIH R01DC012578, T32DC00040, UL1TR002537. Authors declare no competing financial interest.
- **One-sentence finding:** Cross-linked zwitterionic coatings (SBMA or CBMA) grafted onto silicone cut fibrinogen adsorption by up to about 20-fold, macrophage attachment up to 30-fold and fibroblast attachment about 10-fold or more, and cut friction to under 1/10 of bare silicone, with the best balance at roughly 5 to 15% cross-linker, while higher cross-linker makes the gel stiffer, less swollen, but less antifouling and less slippery.

Substrate: medical-grade reinforced PDMS (Bentec Medical) for protein, cell and friction tests; Sylgard 184 PDMS also used. Benzophenone grafting (50 g/L feed). Total monomer 35 wt % in water, PEGDMA (MW 400) cross-linker varied 0 to 100% of monomer (tested: 0, 1.6, about 5, 13, 31.3, 50, 66.7, 100%). UV 30 mW/cm2 at 365 nm. Photografted coating about 40 micrometres thick when hydrated (water-tracing confocal, Supporting Information). All data are in vitro. Mechanical data (swelling, modulus) are from separate free-standing 1 mm thick hydrogel discs, not from the thin coating on PDMS.

### Figure 1 (PDF p. 4, journal p. 4497). Fibrinogen adsorption vs cross-linker percent
- **Shows:** (A to F) heat-map fluorescence images of fibrinogen on uncoated PDMS and CBMA films at 0, 5, 13, 50, 100% cross-linker. (G) relative fibrinogen adsorption (uncoated PDMS = 1.0).
- **Conditions compared:** CBMA vs SBMA; cross-linker 0 to 66.7%; neat PEGDMA (100%); uncoated PDMS.
- **Numbers, exact from text:** "nearly 20-fold reduction" in the abstract; 20-fold reduction at "approximately 5 and 13 wt %" cross-linker; films up to 50% cross-linker still give "an order of magnitude decrease"; the 0% cross-linker film allowed fibrinogen adhesion "roughly equal" to uncoated; the 1% cross-linker film allowed about twice the adhesion of the 5% film; neat PEGDMA adsorbed more than uncoated PDMS; at 67% cross-linker SBMA showed roughly double the adhesion of CBMA.
- **Numbers, read from figure, approximate (relative to uncoated = 1.0):**

| Cross-linker % | CBMA | SBMA |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0.90 | 0.70 |
| 1.6 | 0.10 | 0.16 |
| about 5 | 0.07 | 0.09 |
| 13 | 0.07 | 0.075 |
| 31.3 | 0.155 | 0.13 |
| 50 | 0.13 | 0.13 |
| 66.7 | 0.205 | 0.33 |
| 100 (PEGDMA only, no zwitterion) | 1.55 (error bar about 0.95 to 2.2) | |

  The best plotted values (about 0.07) correspond to about 14-fold, not 20-fold, so the text's "20-fold" is a rounded or best-case value.
- **n:** three samples per cross-link density; nine images per sample. **Statistic:** one-way ANOVA with Tukey is described in Methods; no p values are given in the caption. Error bars SEM.
- **Units:** relative fluorescence, unitless.

### Figure 4 (PDF p. 6, journal p. 4499). Swelling and compressive modulus vs cross-linker percent
- **Shows:** (A) equilibrium swelling percent, in PBS and in FBS (serum). (B) compressive modulus of hydrogels swollen in PBS. Free-standing 1 mm hydrogels, not the coating on PDMS.
- **Conditions:** CBMA vs SBMA; cross-linker 1.6 to 31.3% (the 35 wt % monomer series tested here only up to 31.3%).
- **Numbers, exact from text:** at the lowest cross-link density in PBS CBMA took up "30% greater" fluid than SBMA; CBMA swelling in PBS fell about 4-fold from lowest to highest cross-link density, SBMA about 3-fold; swelling plateaued near 10 to 12% cross-linker; serum-soaked samples swelled less than PBS, especially SBMA at the two lowest cross-link densities; compressive modulus rose 20-fold (SBMA) and 50-fold (CBMA) from lowest to highest cross-linker; highest-cross-linked CBMA modulus "nearly double" SBMA; modulus calculated between 1 and 5% strain; no coating delamination was seen during swelling.
- **Numbers, read from figure, approximate. Swelling (%):**

| Cross-linker % | CBMA PBS | CBMA FBS | SBMA PBS | SBMA FBS |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.6 | 980 | 950 | 750 | 500 |
| 4.8 | 520 | 520 | 400 | 355 |
| 13 | 310 | 300 | 260 | 270 |
| 31.3 | 225 | 225 | 215 | 210 |

  **Modulus (MPa), PBS-swollen:**

| Cross-linker % | CBMA | SBMA |
|---|---|---|
| 1.6 | about 1 (point hidden or overlapping; text implies about 0.7) | 1 |
| 4.8 | 4.8 | 1.1 |
| 13 | 17.5 | 6 |
| 31.3 | 34.7 (error bar about 31.5 to 38) | 18.8 |
- **n:** n = 3 per point for swelling, n = 4 for modulus (caption). Error bars are one standard deviation. **Statistic:** CBMA vs SBMA difference at high cross-linker not significant for swelling (text); none given for modulus.
- **Units:** swelling percent of dry mass, defined as 100 x (wet mass minus dry mass) / dry mass; modulus in megapascals as printed.
- **Caution:** a modulus of 1 to 35 MPa for gels that hold 200 to 1000% water is high for a hydrogel; the authors do not comment on it. Check units with the authors before showing absolute modulus values.

### Figure 5 (PDF p. 7, journal p. 4500). Coefficient of friction
- **Shows:** (A) relative coefficient of friction for four coatings with 4% cross-linker (CBMA, SBMA, PEGMA, and cross-linked PEGDMA with no monomer). (B) CBMA and SBMA vs cross-linker percent. Everything normalized to uncoated PDMS = 1.0.
- **Test:** pin-on-disk tribometer, 6 mm sapphire ball, immersed in PBS, mean over 20 laps; reinforced medical-grade PDMS discs.
- **Numbers, exact from text:** coatings give "an overall 10-fold decrease" in friction vs uncoated PDMS (CBMA, SBMA, PEGMA); CBMA slightly lower than SBMA; PEGMA about double CBMA; neat PEGDMA about half of uncoated PDMS; in panel B friction decreased "up to 20 times"; friction rose with cross-linker for both; at low cross-link CBMA slightly lower, at high cross-link SBMA lower. Uncoated PDMS SD = 0.0430 (caption).
- **Numbers, read from figure, approximate (relative to uncoated = 1.0):**
  - (A) CBMA 0.055; SBMA 0.062; PEGMA 0.085; PEGDMA 0.47 (error bar to about 0.61).
  - (B) 1.6%: CBMA 0.053, SBMA 0.055. About 5%: CBMA 0.060, SBMA 0.063. 13%: CBMA 0.10, SBMA 0.075. 31.3%: CBMA 0.145, SBMA 0.10.
- **Note:** plotted values (0.055 to 0.145) correspond to about 7-fold to 18-fold lower friction. The text's "10-fold" and "up to 20 times" are broadly consistent with this. Absolute friction coefficients are not given.
- **n:** n = 4 per bar or point (caption). SEM. **Statistic:** "significantly decreased" stated in the text; no p values given.

## Numbers quoted in the text

Page numbers are PDF pages (journal page in brackets).

- p. 1 [4494], abstract: "nearly a 20-fold reduction in fibrinogen adsorption" across a wide range of cross-link densities; macrophage count reduced "by 30-fold"; fibroblast count reduced "nearly 10-fold"; more cell adhesion once cross-linker exceeded 50%; friction "dramatically reduced".
- p. 3 [4496], methods: monomer 35 wt % in water, 0.05 wt % Irgacure 2959; example CBMA at 5% cross-linker: 33.25 wt % CBMA, 1.75 wt % PEGDMA, 64.95 wt % water; UV 30 mW/cm2; benzophenone 50 g/L soak 1 h; coating about 40 micrometres thick when hydrated; swelling films 1 mm thick; compression rate 0.5 N/min, modulus between 1 and 5% strain; modulus replicated 4 times; macrophages 7 days; fibroblasts 48 h; 10 images per substrate; fibrinogen 1.0 mg/mL for 1 h, 9 images per sample.
- p. 3 [4496], results: 20-fold protein reduction at about 5 and 13 wt % cross-linker; up to 50% cross-linker still an order of magnitude below uncoated; SBMA about double CBMA protein at 67% cross-linker.
- p. 4 to 5 [4497 to 4498]: macrophage reduction up to 30 times; lowest macrophage density at 1 to 31 wt % cross-linker; fibrocyte density reduced over 10-fold; fibroblast thickness test 20 to 80 micrometres.
- p. 6 [4499]: CBMA 30% more fluid uptake than SBMA at lowest cross-link density in PBS; CBMA swelling 4-fold, SBMA 3-fold lower from lowest to highest cross-link; swelling plateau around 10% cross-linker.
- p. 6 to 7 [4499 to 4500]: modulus 20-fold increase (SBMA) and 50-fold (CBMA); CBMA modulus nearly double SBMA at highest cross-linker; water content of bath 35% vs 52.5% (Supporting Information); friction 10-fold lower with 4% cross-linker, PEGMA about double CBMA, PEGDMA about half of PDMS, up to 20-fold lower in panel B.
- p. 7 to 8 [4500 to 4501]: best antifouling "usually in the range of 5 to 15%" cross-linker; workable range 1 to 50%; modulus rises significantly at 12% cross-linker where swelling plateaus; low adhesion up to about 12%.

## Candidate website charts

1. **"Friction against silicone: bare vs coated"**
   - Chart type: horizontal bars or dot plot, relative coefficient of friction, bare PDMS = 1.0, with CBMA, SBMA, PEGMA and a note on cross-linker level (4%); optional second panel of friction vs cross-linker.
   - Data series: Figure 5A (0.055, 0.062, 0.085) and 5B values above; uncoated = 1.0.
   - Data status: bar values **read from figure, approximate**; "10-fold decrease" and "up to 20-fold" are **exact from text**. Directly relevant to insertion force for catheters, leads and cochlear arrays (the paper states this motivation but measures friction, not insertion force).
2. **"Protein sticking vs how cross-linked the coating is"**
   - Chart type: line or dot chart, relative fibrinogen vs cross-linker percent, CBMA and SBMA, with uncoated = 1.0 reference line and shaded 1 to 50% working window.
   - Data series: Figure 1G values above.
   - Data status: **read from figure**; "20-fold" and "order of magnitude up to 50%" **exact from text**.
3. **"Cell attachment vs cross-linker: macrophages and fibroblasts"**
   - Chart type: two small multiples (macrophage, fibroblast) of cell density vs cross-linker, CBMA and SBMA, uncoated reference.
   - Data series: Figure 2G and 3G tables above.
   - Data status: **read from figure**; "30-fold" and "nearly 10-fold" **exact from text**. Shows the U-shaped result (best at 1 to 31% cross-linker). Macrophages and fibroblasts are the two cell types of the foreign body response.
4. **"The trade-off: stiffer and less swollen vs more antifouling"**
   - Chart type: small multiples or dual-axis chart with cross-linker percent on the x axis and swelling, modulus, relative friction and relative fibroblast density as separate panels.
   - Data series: Figures 4A, 4B, 5B, 3G (cross-linker 1.6 to 31.3%).
   - Data status: **read from figure**; fold changes (20-fold and 50-fold modulus; 4-fold and 3-fold swelling) **exact from text**. Gives a durability and mechanical-properties story. See modulus unit caution above.
5. **"CBMA vs SBMA swelling in saline and in serum"** (supporting)
   - Chart type: grouped bars or lines. Data series: Figure 4A. Data status: **read from figure**; "30% greater" **exact from text**.

Caution for website use: antifouling data are in vitro; the friction test is a sapphire ball on a coated disc in PBS, not a tissue or device test. The paper has no in vivo results and no insertion force data. The mechanical data are from free-standing 1 mm gels.
