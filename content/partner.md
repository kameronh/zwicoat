# Partner with us (page copy)

## Headline

Bring us a device with a fouling, scar or friction problem. We coat it
free and return it with data.

## Three steps (from the decks and white papers)

1. **Feasibility, free.** You send parts. We coat them in our facility,
   with no cap on quantity, and return coated parts with matched
   uncoated controls and data on coverage and adhesion, friction, and
   protein and bacterial adhesion. Your team runs its own tests on the
   coated parts.
2. **Joint development.** We optimize the coating on your production
   devices and run the program the claim needs: ISO 10993
   biocompatibility, sterilization validation on your cycle, accelerated
   aging and shelf life, and an animal model.
3. **Manufacturing and filing.** We coat for you. Our FDA Device Master
   File holds the formulation, process and safety data; through a letter
   of authorization FDA reviews it confidentially and your submission
   incorporates it by reference.

## Who this is for

Device manufacturers (cochlear implants and neurostimulators, breast
implants and expanders, catheters and vascular access, airway and ENT
tubes, GI stents, PEEK implants, sensors), contract and component
manufacturers, consultants and innovation centers bringing client
devices.

## What the regulatory path looks like

A coated device usually needs a new 510(k), or a PMA supplement for a
PMA device. Plan on a Pre-Submission. Performance claims (lower
friction, less protein and bacterial adhesion) rest on bench data;
clinical claims need clinical data. ZwiGuard is non-biocidal, so no
antimicrobial-agent data are required. Expect FDA to treat the
zwitterionic polymer as a novel material: plan on a full ISO 10993
package plus chemical characterization. We are building an ISO 13485
quality system under our NIH Phase II program, ready for partner audit.

## Sterilization and shipping

Steam, EtO, UV and gamma (wet or dry) and dry e-beam: coating intact.
Wet e-beam failed. Bond strength held after gamma or e-beam plus
shipping. Parts ship wet or dry; dry is the default.
