## The numbers (stat tiles, W12 data)

191 experiments with a written report. 1,156 adhesion tests. 468 coated
cochlear implant samples scored. 136 animals with recorded outcomes. 9
peer-reviewed papers and 3 PhD theses. Ten years, 2016 to 2026.

Line under the tiles: Every number on this site traces to a study or a
dataset in our archive. Sources sit under every chart.

## Four properties, one chart each

1. **Less scar.** Capsule thickness 30 to 85 percent lower in every study,
   every species, every duration, on silicone and on real implants. (W04)
2. **Lower impedance.** Working human cochlear implants in sheep read
   about half the impedance of the uncoated ear at implantation and after
   four weeks. (W08)
3. **Easier insertion.** Insertion force about half on cochlear implants,
   endotracheal tubes and probe covers. (W07)
4. **Fewer bacteria, less friction.** 97 percent less S. aureus, 92
   percent less S. epidermidis, more than 90 percent less friction. (W05)

## It stays on

The durability timeline (W06): two weeks in rats to one year in mice, no
loss of antifouling, films intact. One sentence on covalent grafting.

## How we work together

Three steps, from the decks: (1) Feasibility, free: you send parts, we
coat them, with no cap on quantity, and return them with matched
uncoated controls and our data. (2) Joint development: we optimize the
coating on your production devices and run ISO 10993, sterilization,
aging and an animal model. (3) Manufacturing: we coat for you, and our
FDA Device Master File sits behind your submission.
