# Tissue expanders and breast implants

Source: ZwiCoat tissue expander white paper (6-25); MedTech expanders
deck; Horne 2023; Peel 2024.

## Spec strip

- Devices: tissue expanders (510(k) path), smooth silicone breast
  implants (PMA supplement)
- Substrates with archived tests: medical silicone (Sylgard, Bentec,
  implant grades)
- Thickness: about 10 to 100 micrometers (0.4 to 0.65 mm on inflated
  expanders in the 2026 campaign)
- Sterilization: steam, EtO, UV, gamma, dry e-beam
- Mechanical: survived inflation to 200 percent of nominal volume and a
  50 J pendulum impact with no cracking or lifting (ZwiCoat 2026)
- Evidence level: small animal (mouse, rat; six weeks to one year)

## The problem

Every silicone implant is walled off in scar. In nearly every patient
the capsule forms thicker than ideal, which means firmness, distortion
and discomfort; at the severe end it tightens into capsular contracture,
the most common long-term complication of implant surgery, with reported
rates from about 2 to 39 percent. Subclinical biofilm on the implant
feeds the inflammation. Textured surfaces were abandoned after the 2019
BIOCELL recall, and smooth implants carry roughly three times the
contracture rate. For expanders, fibrosis restricts filling and infection
is the leading cause of removal, with rates reported as high as about
29 percent. Antibiotic irrigation, no-touch technique and dermal matrix
wraps act downstream; none change the silicone surface where the
response starts.

## What ZwiGuard does here

- **A thinner capsule on silicone.** Capsule thickness around coated
  silicone fell about two thirds in mice at six weeks (82 to 27
  micrometers; a PEG film gave 43) and about 63 percent in an independent
  cohort (Peel 2024). In rats, low-crosslinker coatings cut capsule
  thickness about 75 to 83 percent at six weeks (dual-network manuscript).
- **It lasts.** Antifouling was unchanged after six months in vivo and the
  film was intact at one year. (Horne 2023)
- **It stretches with the device.** A coated 100 cc expander went to
  150 and then 200 cc, twice, submerged in saline, with no cracking or
  lifting. The same coating survived a 50 J pendulum impact. (ZwiCoat
  2026, internal)
- **Fewer bacteria.** 97 percent less S. aureus and 92 percent less S.
  epidermidis adhesion on coated silicone in vitro, and 95 percent fewer
  viable S. aureus on explants in rats. (Shen 2021)

## Why it drops in

One dip-and-cure step on the finished shell, no new molds. The surface
stays smooth, so the coating does not revisit the texturing question.
Materials under a dollar per device. Compatible with the sterilization
cycles already in use.

## How to start

Expanders first (510(k)), implants second (PMA supplement with clinical
data). Send shells or expanders; we coat them free and return them with
controls and data on coverage, adhesion and stretch. Joint development
adds ISO 10993, repeated-cycle inflation to failure, and an animal
capsule study.
