# PEEK implants and interventional devices

Source: OV white paper; Kyle Ruby PEEK lap-shear data (archive); Terry
Yin cranioplasty collaboration (ongoing).

## Spec strip

- Devices: PEEK cranial and spinal implants, interventional sheaths and
  catheters
- Substrates with archived tests: PEEK, silicone, PVC, parylene-C,
  platinum (titanium, cobalt-chrome: feasibility round)
- Bond: PEEK needs no primer; its backbone ketone acts as the
  photoinitiator. Coated PEEK failed inside the gel, not at the interface
  (about 160 kPa swollen, over 600 kPa dry)
- Coverage: overmolded for full encapsulation, or dip coated
- Sterilization: steam, EtO, gamma, dry e-beam
- Evidence level: bench (PEEK adhesion), small animal (silicone, one
  year); rat cranioplasty study under way

## The problem

Infection caused 25 percent of knee and 15 percent of hip revisions in
US national data, and a two-stage revision for periprosthetic infection
costs more than twice an aseptic one. Staphylococci cause 50 to 60
percent of those infections and resist antibiotics once in a biofilm.
PEEK is hydrophobic, lacks bone contact, and most bacterial strains
tested adhered to it more than to titanium; 15 percent of 183 PEEK
cranioplasties had complications. Antibiotic-loaded bone cement did not
significantly change one-year revision for infection in 1.9 million knee
replacements. On interventional devices, lubricious coatings have been
documented by FDA to peel, flake and shed.

## What ZwiGuard does here

- 97 and 92 percent less staphylococcal adhesion in vitro; 95 percent
  fewer viable S. aureus on rat explants (Shen 2021).
- Capsule 27 micrometers against 82 uncoated on silicone; about 60
  percent thinner on implanted arrays including platinum (Horne 2023).
- Antifouling intact after one year in vivo (Horne 2023).
- PEEK bonds directly and strongly: the bond to PEEK is stronger than the
  gel itself (ZwiCoat 2026, lap-shear).
- More than 90 percent lower friction against tissue, steel and ceramic
  (Bennion 2021).

## Why it drops in

One coating step on existing designs; thickness tunable 1 to 100
micrometers; an automated jig for dip coating and overmolding for full
encapsulation. Drug-free, so no combination product and no stewardship
burden.

## How to start

Send PEEK parts or sheaths. We coat them free and return them with
controls and data on coverage, adhesion, friction and bacterial
adhesion. Joint development adds ISO 10993, a hemocompatibility screen
for blood-contact devices, and an implant study.
