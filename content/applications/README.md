# Application pages

One file per application. Each is the summarized white paper for the
web: about 400 to 600 words, the same five blocks in the same order so
the pages feel like one family, and a figure list drawn from
figures/MANIFEST.md and assets-staging/deck-images/MANIFEST.csv.

Blocks (every page):

1. **Spec strip** (Direction B header): device types, substrates with
   archived tests, coating thickness range, sterilization compatibility,
   regulatory path, evidence level. Rendered as a row of labeled values.
2. **The problem**: three or four sentences with the two or three most
   telling numbers from the white paper, each with its citation.
3. **What ZwiGuard does here**: the claims that apply, each with the
   study and the number; one chart and one real image.
4. **Why it drops in**: substrate, process step, sterilization, cost,
   one-platform note.
5. **How to start**: the free feasibility round, what joint development
   adds, what the Master File covers; the "send us parts" call to action.
6. References (collapsible).

Files:

- neural-interfaces.md (cochlear implants, neurostimulators, BCI)
- tissue-expanders.md (and breast implants)
- vascular-access.md
- indwelling-catheters.md
- gi-stents.md
- peek-and-orthopedic.md
- airway.md
- sensors.md
- ent-tubes.md

Claims follow content/claims-rules.md. Where a white paper claims a
substrate or result the archive does not hold (polyurethane, blood
contact, Gram-negative bacteria), the page says "the feasibility round
measures this on your parts" rather than asserting it.
