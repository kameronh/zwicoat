## Spec strip

- Devices: endotracheal tubes, tracheostomy tubes (PVC)
- Substrates with archived tests: PVC; PVC bonds without benzophenone
- Thickness: set by cure time (about 250 to 375 micrometers at 9 to 15 s
  on ET tubes)
- Sterilization: EtO expected; gamma and dry e-beam tested on other
  substrates
- Regulatory path: 510(k)
- Evidence level: human cadaveric larynx (insertion force), rat tracheal
  study (two weeks), bench

## The problem

83 percent of intubated ICU adults have laryngeal injury. Biofilm forms on
95 percent of endotracheal tubes and each ventilator pneumonia costs about
$40,000. Silver-coated tubes address infection only, and modestly.
Nothing on the market addresses the friction and the foreign body
response that cause ulcers, granulation and stenosis, or the mucus
plugging that blocks tracheostomy tubes. In children each extra five
days of intubation raises subglottic stenosis risk about 50 percent.

## What ZwiGuard does here

- **Gentler intubation.** In a cadaveric larynx on a force transducer,
  two otolaryngologists intubated with coated and uncoated tubes. The
  coating cut integrated insertion force by about 60 percent for both,
  though they differ fivefold in absolute force. (ZwiCoat 2026)
- **Whole tubes, end to end.** Dip coating with the cuff inflated coats
  the full tube; thickness follows cure time (about 254, 335 and 375
  micrometers at 9, 12 and 15 s). (ZwiCoat 2026)
- **Strong bond to PVC without benzophenone.** PVC is the strongest
  substrate in the lap-shear database (median about 240 kPa) and bonds
  with no pretreatment. (Lap-shear database; ETT manuscript)
- **Two-week rat tracheal study.** Histology comparing uncoated and
  coated tubes against no tube; the manuscript carries the readout.
- **Mucus flows off.** 90 to 100 percent less tilt needed to start mucus
  flow on coated sheets; faster plug clearance in tubes. (Horne 2025)

## Why it drops in

One dip step on finished PVC tubes with no pretreatment. Thickness is a
dial. Solvent pretreatments were tested and are not needed.

## How to start

Send tubes. We coat them free and return them with controls, cross
sections and insertion-force data. Joint development adds biofilm and
mucus testing, ISO 10993, EtO validation and an animal model.
