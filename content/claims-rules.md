# Claims rules for zwicoat.com v2

These keep the site honest and keep it out of regulatory trouble. Every
sentence with a number must pass them.

1. **Performance claims only, never clinical claims.** We may say the
   coating cut capsule thickness, bacterial adhesion, friction, insertion
   force or impedance in a named study. We never say it prevents
   infection, prevents capsular contracture, improves hearing, or
   reduces revisions in patients. The white papers say "we make no
   clinical claim"; the site says the same.
2. **Name the model.** Every in-vivo number says the species and the
   duration ("in mice, one year"; "in sheep, four weeks"). Human cochlear
   implant arrays were tested in mice, sheep and cadaveric cochleae; no
   coated device has been implanted in a human. Say so where it matters.
3. **Name the source.** A figure caption ends with the study (author,
   year, journal) or "ZwiCoat internal data, year". Numbers read off
   published figures are "approximately" and rounded.
4. **Round and use a tilde.** "~60 percent", "about half", "1,156 tests".
   No decimals in prose.
5. **Published beats internal.** Lead with peer-reviewed results; mark
   internal results as such. Manuscripts in preparation are "manuscript
   in preparation", never cited as published.
6. **Unverified substrates are not claimed.** The archive holds tests on
   silicone, PVC, PEEK, parylene-C, polyurethane (the CIVCO probe covers;
   some database rows call them polyester, Kam confirmed polyurethane on
   2026-10-29) and platinum contacts. Published adhesion data are on
   silicone (Leigh 2019); the others are ZwiCoat internal tests and are
   labeled as such. Titanium, cobalt-chrome, PTFE and nitinol are untested:
   the site says "the feasibility round answers this on your parts".
7. **Competitor data are quoted from the trials, with citations**
   (CATHETER, PICNIC, antibiotic cement registry), and never editorialized
   beyond what the white papers already say.
8. **Patents by number** (US 11,525,069; 11,814,526; 12,116,498; plus
   Australia, China, Japan, Mexico). Funding by program (NIH SBIR Phase I
   and Phase II). No valuations, no market-size projections on the site.
9. **No testimonials we do not have.** No quotes from partners unless
   they approved the quote in writing.
10. **The trademark** is ZwiGuard (with the mark on first use per page).
    The company is ZwiCoat Materials Innovations; ZCMI is the short form.
