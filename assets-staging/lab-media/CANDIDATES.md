## Pendulum video

Source: `ZD\Kyle Ruby\26.10 Tissue expanders and pendulum\` (Kyle Ruby, October 2026; deck "Impact Testing of Hydrogel-Coated Tissue Expanders"). Test: weighted pendulum (steel hitch ball, optional 10 lb plate) strikes a dip-coated device mounted on a backboard. Phase 1 used a 200 cc sizer (about 12 to 54 J), Phase 2 used a Mentor 125 cc oblong expander (clean padded hit at about 44 to 54 J, no visible coating damage) and a Mentor Artoura 200 cc (59 to 70 J, small localized scrape). Energy values are the deck's approximations. Deck says "no visible damage", so site copy must stay at that wording.

- **Synced impact - side by side v2.mp4 (copied as pendulum-sidebyside-v2.mp4).** This is the synced two-panel clip. Left panel is the close-up of the expander on the backboard, right panel is the wide view of the rig and the swinging arm. 1080x1080, 5.5 s, 60 fps, no audio stream, no burned-in captions. The only text is a tiny handwritten blue-tape label on the backboard (about "46 J") that is visible in the frame. The deck calls the close-up "slow motion"; the file plays at 60 fps and I could not confirm from the file alone whether the close-up has been slowed. This is the one to use.
- **Synced impact - side by side.mp4 (v1, copied as pendulum-sidebyside-v1-labeled.mp4).** Older cut, 1224x1080, 6.8 s, panels swapped (overview left, close-up right) with burned-in "Overview" and "Impact close-up" labels. No audio. A copy sits at the folder root too. Superseded by v2.
- **Impact video.mp4 and Overview video.mp4.** Raw source clips for the same hit, each 720x1280 portrait. Impact: 5.9 s at 59.94 fps (close-up of the expander against the backboard, ball and weighted arm swing in and rebound). Overview: 5.1 s at 30 fps (wide view of the black backboard rig). Both have no audio. These are the two inputs the side-by-side was built from. Identical bytes to the slide 11 clips in the pptx.
- **Synced impact video.pptx and Synced impact video v2.pptx.** Each holds only the matching side-by-side mp4 and a title image (no new stills).
- **Impact Testing - Updated.pptx** (17 MB) embeds 13 more mp4 clips, one pair (slow-motion close-up plus overview) per test. All are copied above, 0.4 to 1.4 MB each, none with audio. The deck stills (the images above) are all in this pptx too.

Recommendation for the site: a silent 10 to 20 s loop, autoplay muted, `playsinline`, no controls, with a poster still (`pendulum-sidebyside-v2-poster.jpg` or `pendulum-impact-moment-02.jpg`). The side-by-side v2 file is only 5.5 s and 1.7 MB, so it can play as is. Options to reach 10 to 20 s without new footage: (1) play v2 twice with a short freeze on the rebound frame (about 12 s), or (2) concatenate v2 (Mentor 125, clean, no damage) with the Artoura 59 to 70 J close-up (8.8 s) for about 14 s, with a caption naming the energy of each hit. Option 2 shows the most severe hit but that test did leave a small scrape, so the caption must say so. Strip captions; add the energy text in HTML, not in the video. Encode to H.264 plus a WebM copy, under 2 MB.

Caveats: the clips are phone video with hands and a messy lab at the edges of the wide shot (red bins, wood pallet, tape label). That is acceptable for a test video, but Kam should confirm it is OK to show the lab surroundings. The expander is small in the wide frame; the close-up carries the story.

## Dip-coating process video

The slot needs a real video of the dip-coating process (not the CAD render). Two phone clips from `ZD\Kyle Ruby\26.04.22_Kyle_DipCoating_ET_Tube\raw data\`. Neither is a clean "jig" video. I did not find any other dip-coating video in the surveyed folders (the other April-May tissue-expander clips, IMG_8353, IMG_8368, IMG_8376, IMG_8377, IMG_8388, show hand dips and curing, and are shorter or cluttered; they were not copied).

- **IMG_8336.mp4 (dipcoat-ett-raw-01.mp4).** 142.9 s, 56 MB, 1080x1920 portrait, 30 fps, with audio. Shows the real process: an ET tube on a rod is lowered into a clear cylinder of monomer, then lifted into a blue LED curing box and held in the light. The frames I sampled show blue-purple UV light, gloved fingers and a sleeve at the top of the frame at several points, exposed wood and heat-sink hardware, and a handheld camera moving around. It is the right content, with rough production quality. Needs trimming to about 12 to 20 s (the first dip and the move into the light, roughly the first 15 to 30 s), muting, and possibly a brightness/crop pass; I did not edit anything. Vertical 9:16 also means a portrait player or a crop to a square.
- **IMG_8345.mp4 (dipcoat-ett-raw-02-cuff-inflate.mp4).** 18.9 s, 6.9 MB, 1080x1920, with audio. This is the finished blue-coated tube cuff being inflated and deflated against a gray wall, clean light, no hands in frame. It is not a dipping video. It is excellent for the airway page and could stand in if Kam accepts "the coated product" instead of "the process".

Recommendation: use IMG_8336 trimmed to 12 to 20 s, muted, with `dipcoat-ett-raw-02-poster-t05s.jpg` or a better frame from 8336 as the poster, as a stopgap until a purpose-shot video exists. Put IMG_8345 on the airway page as a separate silent loop. If Kam wants the process video to look like a product demo, a new shoot is still the better answer; 8336 reads as lab footage.
