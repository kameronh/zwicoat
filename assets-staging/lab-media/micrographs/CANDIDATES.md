# Micrograph candidates for coated CI arrays (worker U)

Searched 2026-10-29. Archive is read-only; files below are unaltered copies (jpg bytes copied as-is; png converted to jpg, quality 92; no crops, no retouching). Source root `ZD` = `C:\Users\Kameron\Zwicoat Dropbox\ZwiCoat Data`.

Important caveats for whoever builds the pages:

- No file here carries a scale bar. The stereo pictures are 1280x720 USB-scope captures with no calibration. The T171/T172 sections have a known core: PDMS tubing OD 0.635 mm (per E-report 25.11.19), so a scale can be added in CSS or in the figure caption if Kam approves.
- "Much better" than the cryosection ring does not mean higher resolution here: the best whole-array stereo views are only 1280x720. The 2816x2112 cryosection pictures are the only high-resolution micrographs in the archive. If Kam wants sharper stereo pictures, they have to be re-shot (or exist outside the Dropbox archive, for example on the scope PC).
- The stereo pictures are of a Cochlear Slim Straight array (per the 7/8 deck slide title) and the T171/T172 sections are PDMS CI proxies; the EA22 section is a real EA22 array. None is a MED-EL array. Caption accordingly ("cochlear implant electrode array" or "CI proxy").
- Blue is methylene blue dye in the coating (lab dye test), not the product's colour. Say so in the caption.
- Parker's e-mail of 2026-03-19 says the earlier first-removal-test coating "was one of the bad outcomes". The 07-08 session pictures below are a later session; Parker or Kam should confirm the coating quality before any "good coating" claim is attached to them.

## Top three and why

1. `ci-array-stereo-tip-01.jpg` (and its twin `-02`). It is exactly what "under the microscope" should look like: a real array tip, every contact visible through an even blue film, nothing else in frame. Weakness: 1280x720, so use it at about 1280 px wide or less and do not full-bleed it on large screens.
2. `ci-proxy-section-blue-ring-01.jpg`. The sharpest and cleanest cross-section in the archive, 2816x2112, with the film clearly visible as a dark blue ring on the carrier. It is better than the cryosection ring currently in use because the coating is saturated and the section has no bubbles. Caption it as a CI proxy section, not an array.
3. `ci-array-section-ea22-01.jpg`. The only high-resolution section that shows a real array body (platinum wire bundle) inside the coating. Busier and less pretty than #2, so use it at a smaller size beside the ring picture as the "this is a real array" proof.

Runner-up: `ci-array-whole-blue-01.jpg` as a narrow strip on the home page.
